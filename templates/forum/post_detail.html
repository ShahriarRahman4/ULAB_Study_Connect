By Master Theorem, a=2, b=2, f(n)=O(n). Since logb(a)=1, case 2 applies and T(n)=Θ(n log n). You pay linear merge work at each of log n levels of the recursion tree.

Recurrence
T(n) = 2T(n/2) + Θ(n)
     = Θ(n log n)
