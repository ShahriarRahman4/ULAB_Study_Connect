Our Vision

To create a collaborative learning environment where every ULAB student can thrive through shared knowledge, structured planning, and peer support.

Our Mission

Empower students with cutting-edge tools for study planning, knowledge sharing, and academic resource management - all in one integrated platform.

Key Features

  • Forum: Q&A and discussion platform with voting and accepted answers
  • Notes System: Markdown notes, PDF uploads, annotations, AI summarization, quiz generation
  • Routine Management: Excel-based exam routine import, personalized saved routines
  • Study Planner: AI-generated study plans based on exam dates and difficulty levels
  • User Profiles: Department and semester-based personalization
