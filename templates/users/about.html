Our story

A campus platform,
not a generic study app

ULAB StudyConnect started as a hackathon idea from students who were tired of scattered routines, lost PDFs, and group chats that bury the one answer you need.

01

Built on campus problems

Exam dates, course codes, and peer notes are first-class — because that's how ULAB actually studies.

02

Designed as a product

Premium UI, clear flows, and tools that feel like a startup — not a class project dump.

03

Owned by students

Every feature exists because a ULABian needed it during a real semester crunch.
